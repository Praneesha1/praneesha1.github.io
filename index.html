I am Gajadi Praneesha. I study Mechanical Engineering at IIT Jodhpur. I am in my third year. I like to design things that move.

My main tool is Fusion 360. I model each part, join the parts into an assembly, and check the motion. Then I make drawings so the part can be made. I also know SolidWorks, AutoCAD, ANSYS Workbench, and Matlab.

On this site every model is real. The engine and the ornithopter are the same files I built in Fusion 360. You can turn them, pull them apart, cut them open, and run the mechanism in your browser.

Outside class I help run campus events and mentor first year students. I am from Hyderabad.

How I work

From sketch to drawing
Step 1

Model the parts

Each part gets its own file. I keep the sketches simple and fully defined so changes are easy later.

Step 2

Build the assembly

I join the parts with the right joints. Revolute where things turn. Slider where things slide. Rigid where things stay put.

Step 3

Check the motion

I run a motion study. If a part hits another part, I go back and fix the geometry.

Step 4

Make the drawings

Front, top, side, section, and exploded views on a sheet with a title block. Ready for a workshop.
