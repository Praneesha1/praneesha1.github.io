Overview

This is a set of six parts I modeled in Fusion 360. I made them to practice the tools. Each one taught me something new. The hook taught me Loft. The pipe elbow taught me Sweep. The hinge taught me how two simple parts work together.

These parts are shown as pictures. The hinge has a short video of it turning.

The six parts

In the order I made them.

Parts 1 to 6
01

Wall mounted hook Loft

It looks simple. The smooth curve between the hook and the base was the hard part. I used the Loft tool to make it. After a few tries I saw why Loft is so useful for curved shapes.

02

Pipe support bracket Course test

This was a course test. It mixes many tools in one part. Extrude, holes, a clamp ring, and a bolt flange. It was a good revision of the basics.

03

Pipe elbow assembly Sweep

I used the Sweep tool here. A flat circle follows a curved path to make the pipe. Watching it turn into a real pipe part showed me how fast CAD tools can be. Sweep became my favorite tool.

04

Flanged elbow pipe fitting Flanges

This is the same elbow with a bolt flange on the end. Flanges like this are used in factory piping. Getting the sizes exact mattered a lot.

05

Flanged support spacer Alignment

The shape is simple. Keeping all the holes lined up took care. Simple looking parts are not always simple to model.

06

Hinge Joints · Motion

Two leaves and a pin. I thought it would be the easiest one. It turned out to be a good lesson in how simple parts fit together. A hinge is something nobody notices until it stops working.
